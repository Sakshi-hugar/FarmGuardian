About FarmGuardian

AI-powered precision weed management, from a single photo to a field-wide plan.

What it is

FarmGuardian isn't just a weed photo classifier. It detects crops and weeds, measures how bad an infestation is, maps where it's concentrated, recommends what to do about it, tracks a field's condition over scans, and is architected to eventually drive an autonomous weed-removal machine — so treatment can be targeted instead of blanket-applied across an entire field.

Why it matters

  • Cuts manual field-walking time to identify weed pressure
  • Targets treatment to dense zones instead of the whole field
  • Reduces unnecessary herbicide use and cost
  • Gives a track record of field health over time
⚠ Prototype notice — This build simulates AI detections for demo purposes (no model is trained/run in this artifact). The pipeline, UI and recommendation logic are fully wired up and designed to swap in a real trained YOLO model with no change to the workflow.
⚠ On chemical use — FarmGuardian never recommends a specific chemical from an image alone. Any treatment suggestion depends on crop type, weed species, growth stage, and local agricultural guidance — always confirm with an agronomist or the product label before applying anything.
